**App name (max 30):** Fast Gallery

**Short description (max 80):**

**Category:** Photography (ya Tools)

**Full description (max 4000):**

Fast Gallery is a fast, offline gallery for the photos and videos on your phone. It has no ads, no tracking and no internet access, so your media stays on your device.

Core features
• Browse all your photos and videos by date, with a fast scroller and pinch-to-zoom grid
• Albums, search, and sort/filter by date, name, size, photos, videos, GIF and RAW previews
• Full-screen viewer with zoom, slideshow, details and EXIF info
• Built-in video player: double-tap to seek 10 seconds, hold for 2x speed, swipe for brightness and volume, picture-in-picture
• Photo editor: crop, rotate, flip, straighten, brightness, contrast, saturation, filters, undo and before/after compare. Edits are saved as a new copy, your original stays untouched
• Select many items at once (drag to select, or select a whole day), then share, favorite, copy, move or delete with progress
• Favorites, Trash with restore, hide albums, and lock albums with your fingerprint or screen lock
• Swipe between tabs, dark mode, English and Hindi

Why Fast Gallery needs photo and video access
Fast Gallery is a gallery app. To show your library, albums and favorites, and to let you manage and edit your media, it needs access to the photos and videos on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.
