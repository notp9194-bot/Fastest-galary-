Fast Gallery ("the app") is a gallery app for viewing, organising and editing the photos and videos stored on your device. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Short version

  • Fast Gallery does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
  • The app has no internet permission and contains no ads, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • Your photos and videos never leave your device because of Fast Gallery.

Information the app accesses

Photos and videos on your device. The app asks for permission to read the photos and videos on your device (and, on Android 14+, you can choose to share only selected items). It uses this access only to show them to you, and to carry out actions you request: share, rename, copy, move, edit, favourite, move to trash, delete, set as wallpaper. Everything happens on your device.

Edited and copied files. When you edit or copy an item, the app saves a new file on your device (for example under Pictures/FastGallery/Edited/). The original is not changed by editing.

Device authentication. If you lock an album, the app asks Android to verify you with your screen lock or biometrics. The verification is done by Android. The app does not receive, see or store your fingerprint, face data, PIN or password – it only learns whether the check succeeded.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of settings data in its private storage on your device: your favourites, trash markers, hidden/locked/pinned album identifiers, theme, grid size, sort and filter choices, slideshow and video preferences, and the last playback position of recently watched videos. It also keeps a temporary image cache to load thumbnails faster. This data stays on your device, is not backed up to any cloud by the app, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Sharing

The app shares nothing by itself. When you choose Share, Android's share sheet sends the selected file to the app you pick. That app's own privacy policy applies from that point.

The app has no access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or the internet.

Children

Fast Gallery is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Security

Because data is not transmitted, the main protection is Android's own security. Album lock restricts opening an album inside the app; it does not encrypt files or hide them from other apps.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
